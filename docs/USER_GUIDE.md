## Introduction

### What is Ummah Flow?

Ummah Flow is a platform designed by Muslims, for Muslims – a digital marketplace where you can:

- **Discover** halal businesses, mosques, Islamic schools, and community services
- **Support** Muslim entrepreneurs and community initiatives
- **Connect** with your local Ummah
- **Share** beneficial resources with your brothers and sisters

Our mission is to strengthen the Muslim community by making it easier to find and support each other's services and businesses, all while ensuring transparency and barakah (blessing) in every transaction.

### Key Features

✨ **Search & Discover** - Find halal restaurants, Islamic bookstores, mosques, and more  
💾 **Save Favorites** - Bookmark providers you love for easy access  
📍 **Location-Based Search** - Find services near you or search nationwide  
🎯 **Category Filters** - Browse by specific categories like Food, Education, Health, etc.  
🤲 **Community Services** - Discover charitable initiatives and social projects  
⚡ **Quick Import** - Business owners can quickly add their listings via Google or Instagram

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

**As-Salamu-Aleikum!** Welcome to the Ummah. Let's get you set up:

#### Sign Up (New Users)

1. **Navigate to Signup**
   - Visit [ummahflow.com](https://ummahflow.com)
   - Click on **"Registrieren"** (Register) or the Profile icon
   - Select **"Noch kein Konto? Jetzt registrieren"** (Don't have an account? Register now)

2. **Enter Your Details**
   - **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address
   - **Password**: Create a secure password (minimum 6 characters)
   - **Confirm Password**: Re-enter your password
   
3. **Review Terms**
   - By continuing, you agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
   - These ensure halal practices and protect your data

4. **Check Your Email**
   - After clicking **"Registrieren"**, check your email inbox
   - You'll receive a confirmation email from Ummah Flow
   - Click the confirmation link to activate your account
   - *Tip: Check your spam folder if you don't see the email within a few minutes*

5. **Email Confirmed - You're Ready!**
   - Once confirmed, you can log in and start exploring
   - May Allah accept your good intentions!

#### Sign In (Returning Users)

1. Visit the **Login** page
2. Enter your **email** and **password**
3. Click **"Anmelden"** (Sign In)
4. If you forgot your password, click **"Passwort vergessen?"** (Forgot password?)

**Note**: If you haven't confirmed your email yet, you'll be prompted to do so before accessing full features.

### Understanding the Interface

#### Mobile Navigation (Bottom Bar)

Ummah Flow is optimized for mobile use. You'll find four main sections in the bottom navigation:

1. **🏠 Home** - Landing page with featured providers and categories
2. **🔍 Search** - Explore and search for providers and services  
3. **➕ Create** - Add your own business or recommend others
4. **👤 Profile** - Your account, saved items, and settings

#### Desktop Navigation (Top Bar)

On desktop, you'll see a clean navigation menu at the top with quick access to:
- Search functionality
- Your profile
- Language selection (Deutsch/English)

## For Seekers - Finding Services

### Browsing the Marketplace

#### Home Page

The home page welcomes you with:

- **Hero Section**: "Von Muslimen für Muslime" (From Muslims, for Muslims)
- **Category Gallery**: Visual cards showcasing different service categories
- **Featured Providers**: Highlighted businesses and services in your area

#### Categories Available

Ummah Flow supports various categories including:

- 📖 **Books & Media** - Islamic literature and educational materials

### Searching for Providers

#### Using the Search Bar

1. **Access Search**
   - Tap the **Search icon** (🔍) in the bottom navigation
   - Or visit the **Providers** page from the homepage

2. **Enter Your Query**
   - Type what you're looking for (e.g., "Halal Restaurant", "Mosque", "Islamic School")
   - The search looks through business names, descriptions, and addresses

3. **Filter by Category**
   - Click the **Category dropdown** to select a specific category
   - Choose **"Alle"** (All) to see everything
   - Categories update dynamically based on available content

4. **Filter by Location**
   - Click the **Location dropdown** to select a city
   - Choose **"Überall"** (Everywhere) to search nationwide
   - Select **"Online"** to find online-only businesses
   - Location options show only cities with active providers

5. **Clear Search**
   - Click the **X icon** in the search bar to clear your query
   - Reset filters by selecting "Alle" and "Überall"

#### Understanding Search Results

Each provider card displays:

- **Provider Image**: Visual preview of the business
- **Category Badge**: Type of service (e.g., Moschee, Café)
- **Provider Name**: Business or service name
- **Address**: Physical location or "Online"
- **Barakah Tags** (if applicable): Special attributes like "Halal", "Zakat", "Sunnah"
- **Save Button** (Heart icon): Bookmark for later

### Viewing Provider Details

#### Opening a Provider Page

- **On Mobile**: Tap any provider card to view full details
- **On Desktop**: Click on a provider card

#### What You'll See

1. **Image Gallery**
   - Swipe through multiple images of the provider
   - View the storefront, products, or services
   - Use arrow buttons or swipe gestures

2. **Provider Information**
   - **Name**: Business or service name
   - **Category**: Type of service
   - **Address**: Full address with navigation option
   - **Description**: Detailed information about the business

3. **Contact Options**
   - **📞 Call**: Tap to call the provider directly
   - **🌐 Website**: Visit their website
   - **📷 Instagram**: View their Instagram profile
   - **📧 Email**: Send them an email

4. **What They Offer**
   - **"Wir bieten"** (We Offer): Services, products, or offerings available
   - View as expandable lists for easy reading

5. **What They Need**
   - **"Wir brauchen"** (We Need): Items or support the provider is seeking
   - Especially relevant for community services and charitable organizations

6. **Community Services** (if applicable)
   - Related charitable initiatives or social projects
   - Connected community programs

7. **Barakah Effects** (if applicable)
   - Special Islamic attributes or certifications
   - Examples: Halal-certified, Sadaqah accepted, Zakat eligible

### Saving & Bookmarking

#### How to Save Providers

1. **From Search Results**
   - Tap the **Heart icon** on any provider card
   - The heart fills in, and you'll see **"Allahuma Barik"** (May Allah bless) animation
   - The provider is now saved to your collection

2. **From Provider Detail Page**
   - Tap the **"Speichern"** (Save) button at the bottom
   - Same blessed animation appears
   - Provider is added to your saved list

#### Viewing Saved Providers

1. Navigate to **Profile** → **Saved** page
2. You'll see all your bookmarked providers and community services
3. Use search and location filters to organize your saved items
4. Tap **"Unsave"** button to remove from your collection

**Pro Tip**: Save providers you visit regularly or want to support in the future. May Allah grant barakah in your choices!

### Navigating to Providers

#### Getting Directions

1. On the provider detail page, tap the **address**
2. Your device will open the default maps application
3. Get turn-by-turn directions to the location

**Note**: Navigation only works for providers with complete physical addresses.

## For Providers - Listing Your Business

### Why List on Ummah Flow?

By adding your business to Ummah Flow, you:

- **Reach Muslim Customers**: Connect with your target audience
- **Support the Ummah**: Make halal services easily discoverable
- **Build Trust**: Showcase your Islamic values and practices
- **Gain Visibility**: Be found by Muslims searching for services like yours
- **Earn Barakah**: Help your community while growing your business, in sha'Allah

### Creating Your Provider Listing

#### Option 1: Quick Import (Recommended) ⚡

The fastest way to get started! Import your business details automatically.

##### Using Google Places

1. **Navigate to Create**
   - Tap the **➕ icon** in the bottom navigation
   - Select **"Quick Import (Beta)"** if available

2. **Choose Google Method**
   - Tap **"Google"** import option
   - You'll see a search interface

3. **Search for Your Business**
   - Enter your business name
   - Results will show matching Google Places listings

4. **Select Your Business**
   - Tap on the correct listing
   - Ummah Flow automatically imports:
     - Business name
     - Full address
     - Phone number
     - Website (if available)

5. **Review & Customize**
   - You'll be taken to a review page
   - Add additional details:
     - Category
     - Description
     - Islamic attributes (Halal certification, etc.)
     - Social media links
   - Upload additional images

6. **Publish**
   - Tap **"Veröffentlichen"** (Publish)
   - Your provider is now live on Ummah Flow!

##### Using Instagram Import

1. **Navigate to Create** → **Quick Import**
2. **Choose Instagram Method**
   - Tap **"Instagram"** import option

3. **Enter Instagram Username**
   - Type your Instagram handle (without @)
   - Tap **"Import"**

4. **Auto-Fill Magic**
   - Ummah Flow imports:
     - Business name
     - Biography/Description
     - Website link
     - Business email
     - Business phone
     - Business address (if available on Instagram)

5. **Complete Missing Details**
   - Add or edit any missing information
   - Select your category
   - Add what you offer and need

6. **Publish Your Listing**

**Note**: Quick Import is a Beta feature. If you encounter issues, use the traditional form method below.

## Community Features

### Community Services vs. Providers

Ummah Flow supports two types of listings:

#### 1. Providers (Businesses)
- Regular businesses and services
- Examples: Restaurants, shops, schools, clinics
- Focus on products and services for sale/hire

#### 2. Community Services (Social Initiatives)
- Charitable organizations
- Non-profit initiatives
- Community programs
- Examples: Zakat distribution, food banks, refugee support, Islamic education programs

**Key Difference**: Community Services focus on serving the Ummah, often through volunteer work, donations, or free services.

### Supporting Community Services

#### Finding Community Services

1. Use the search function
2. Filter by community service categories (e.g., "Zakat", "Charity")
3. Look for providers marked with community service badges

#### Getting Involved

On a community service detail page, you'll see:

- **What They Offer**: Programs and services available
- **What They Need**: How you can help
  - Volunteer opportunities
  - Donation needs
  - Professional services needed

**Ways to Support**:
- 💰 **Donate**: Give zakat, sadaqah, or direct donations
- 🙋 **Volunteer**: Offer your time and skills
- 📢 **Share**: Spread the word to your network
- 🤲 **Make Dua**: Pray for the success of the initiative

**Hadith**: "Whoever relieves a Muslim of a burden from the burdens of the world, Allah will relieve him of a burden from the burdens on the Day of Judgment." - *Sahih Muslim*

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Is Ummah Flow free to use?**  
A: Yes! Ummah Flow is completely free for both seekers and providers. We're committed to serving the Muslim community with no fees, in sha'Allah.

**Q: Do I need an account to browse providers?**  
A: No, you can browse and search without an account. However, you'll need an account to save providers, create listings, or access personalized features.

**Q: How does Ummah Flow ensure businesses are halal?**  
A: We rely on the Islamic principle of trust (amanah). Providers self-report their halal status. We encourage users to verify certifications and report any concerns. Community feedback helps maintain authenticity.

**Q: Can I list a business outside Germany?**  
A: Absolutely! While we started in Germany, Ummah Flow welcomes providers from anywhere in the world. We're building a global Muslim marketplace, in sha'Allah.

**Q: How do I report inappropriate content?**  
A: Contact our support team (see Support section below) with details. We take reports seriously and investigate promptly.

### For Seekers

**Q: How do I know if a restaurant is truly halal?**  
A: Check the provider's description for halal certification details. Look for certification badges in the Barakah Effects section. When in doubt, contact the provider directly or ask for proof of certification.

**Q: Can I leave reviews for providers?**  
A: Review functionality is coming soon, in sha'Allah! For now, you can save providers and share them with friends.

**Q: What does "Online" mean in location filter?**  
A: It shows businesses that operate exclusively online (e-commerce stores, online tutoring, etc.) without a physical location.

**Q: Why isn't my city showing in the location filter?**  
A: Location options are dynamically generated from active providers. If your city isn't listed, there aren't any providers there yet. Consider adding the first one!

### For Providers

**Q: How long does it take for my listing to go live?**  
A: Listings go live immediately after you publish. No approval process needed!

**Q: Can I edit my listing after publishing?**  
A: Yes! Visit your Profile, tap your provider, and use the Edit button to make changes anytime.

**Q: Can I add multiple locations for my business?**  
A: Currently, each listing supports one location. If you have multiple branches, create separate listings for each location.

**Q: What if I don't have professional photos?**  
A: Start with smartphone photos! Good lighting and clear images work great. You can always update photos later as you get better ones.

**Q: How do customers contact me?**  
A: Through the contact methods you provide (phone, email, website, social media). Make sure your contact information is current!

**Q: Can I see how many people viewed my listing?**  
A: Analytics features are coming soon, in sha'Allah! Currently, you can see how many users saved your provider (bookmark count).

**Q: Is there a limit to how many providers I can create?**  
A: No limit! Create as many listings as you need.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Which browsers does Ummah Flow support?**  
A: Ummah Flow works on all modern browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). We recommend using the latest version for the best experience.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**  
A: Ummah Flow is a Progressive Web App (PWA), optimized for mobile browsers. You can "Add to Home Screen" for an app-like experience. Native apps may come in the future, in sha'Allah.

**Q: Why isn't my confirmation email arriving?**  
A: 
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Verify you entered the correct email address
- Wait a few minutes (sometimes emails are delayed)
- Request a new confirmation email from the login page

**Q: The website isn't loading properly. What should I do?**  
A:
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies
2. Try a different browser
3. Check your internet connection
4. Contact support if the issue persists
