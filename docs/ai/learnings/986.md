---
title: Same-origin referrer does not mean there is history to go back to
issue: 986
---

**Learning:** A link middle-clicked into a new tab from uFlow has a same-origin `document.referrer` but no history, so "can I `router.back()`?" has to come from comparing against the pathname the tab entered on, not from the referrer.
